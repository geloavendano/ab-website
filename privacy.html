How we handle your personal data

Angat Pinas, Inc. (Angat Buhay) follows the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). This notice explains what we collect on this website, why, and what you can ask of us.

What we collect

  • Volunteer sign-ups: name, email, mobile number (optional), region, advocacies of interest and organization (optional).
  • Partnership and donation emails: what you choose to send us, including proof of transfer for receipts.
  • Website analytics: page views and device type, counted without cookies and without identifying you.

Report and toolkit downloads do not require any personal details.

Why we use it

  • To match volunteers with programs and contact them about activities.
  • To respond to partnership inquiries.
  • To issue donation receipts and BIR certificates, which the law requires us to keep.
  • To understand which pages help people, so we can improve the site.

How long we keep it

DataKept for
Volunteer sign-ups with no activity12 months, then deleted

Who can see it

Only Angat Buhay staff who need it for the purposes above, using role-based accounts. We do not sell or share your data for marketing. Service providers that host our website and email process data on our behalf under contract.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, download or delete your data, object to its use, or withdraw consent at any time. You can also complain to the National Privacy Commission.
