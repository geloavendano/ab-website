Sky on film

Blurred sky, film grain and crayon doodles for the big moments; calm paper and clean cards for everything people actually read.

Principles

How the mood and the components share a page without fighting.

Two registers

Sky + grain + doodles are for hero moments: the homepage hero, one contained panel per page, and the Donate card. Everything else sits on warm paper.

One loud thing per screen

Brand pink is the attention colour. Use it for Donate (or the one action a page exists for), never for two buttons in the same view.

Doodles reward, they don’t decorate

On calm surfaces a doodle only appears when something happens: it draws in on scroll, or under a title on hover. One scribble per section title, max.

Colour as a signal, not as text

Brand teal and pink fail contrast as text. Pillar colours live in underlines and accents; text uses navy, ink, or the darker teal/pink steps.

Surfaces

The sky is pure CSS (stacked radial gradients, blurred and slowly drifting) with an SVG noise tile on top, so it scales to any size and weighs nothing. Grain and drift stop for people who prefer reduced motion.

Doodles

Hand-drawn SVG paths, roughened by a shared “crayon” filter (wobble + grit). They take any brand colour, scale cleanly, and can draw themselves in. Hover a tile to redraw it. From the guide’s element list: arrows, swirls, sparkles, crown, rays, encircle, speech bubble, spring, zigzag, underline swashes.
