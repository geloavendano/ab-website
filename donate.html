After you donate

  1. Send proof to donations@angatbuhay.ph with your full name, address, and tax identification number (TIN).
  2. We will send a donation receipt / acknowledgement receipt within 5 working days.
  3. Need a BIR Certificate of Donation (Form 2322) for tax deduction? Mention it in the same email and we'll send it with your receipt. PLACEHOLDER
