Design notes

      Key journeys (from the wireframe)

      1. Home hero → Watch more → Stories feed → a story
      2. Home → an advocacy → its projects and impact stories → "See other stories" (pre-filtered feed)
      3. Advocacy → The team → "Meet the rest of the team" → About
      4. Home → Presence map → region/province table
      5. Home → Be involved → Donate / Volunteer / Fundraisers

      About the content

      Story titles, authors and dates, advocacy descriptions, team names, mission/vision and certifications come from angatbuhay.ph. Story formats, project/area tags, project dates, volunteer organizations, fundraiser details and items tagged SAMPLE are illustrative. Bank account numbers are deliberately left out of the prototype.
